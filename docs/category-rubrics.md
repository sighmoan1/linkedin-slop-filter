# Category rubric revision: 2026-10-10

The revised defaults judge the substance of extracted post text. They do not claim to detect AI authorship. Each category has an independent definition; an advert can also teach something useful, and a sincere personal update can match no category.

This revision preserves the existing actions and strict `score > threshold` comparison. Saved definitions, including previous defaults, stay unchanged. Fresh installs use the new definitions; existing users adopt them through an explicit default reset rather than an automatic rewrite.

The implementation and reproducible synthetic comparison are being prepared under issue #45. Development cases were used to refine the rubric; fresh validation cases were evaluated after it was frozen. Neither corpus is a representative accuracy benchmark, and local results do not establish cloud-provider quality.
