    Data sent for checks

    Text checks send post text to your selected provider. Optional image checks also send resized attachments and captions to OpenRouter. Messages, comments, profile details and cookies are not collected. Keys stay in this browser profile and never go to the LinkedIn page. Scores cannot prove who wrote a post.

    Storage and keys

    Saved scores contain a post fingerprint and scores, without post text. They are kept for up to 30 days and 2,000 posts. Ollama scores last until the browser session ends. Changing the provider, model, address or definitions triggers new checks.

    How this is calculated

    A wrong match means a rule applies to a category you did not choose. A missed match means you chose a category but its score is below or exactly at the threshold.

    Only the latest correction for each identical post counts, using the current provider and definitions. Examples with missing scores are excluded. Analysis uses saved settings; unsaved edits are not used.

    A suggestion needs at least five chosen and five unchosen scored examples. It reduces disagreements without increasing wrong matches on those same examples, but may perform worse on other posts. It changes only your draft until you save.

    Data saved with a correction

    On a post, choose Review scores, select the categories that fit, then Save correction. This saves the post text and your choices in this browser profile. Showing a post or checking its scores does not save text here. Corrections stay local and do not train the model or change your rules.
