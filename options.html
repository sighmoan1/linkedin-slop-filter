Less slop. More substance.

Fold formulaic LinkedIn feed posts. Reveal any post with one click.

When enabled, visible feed post text is sent to your selected API provider. Messages, comments and profile details are not collected. Keys stay in this Chrome profile's extension storage; they are not synced. Classification is a style judgement, not proof of AI authorship. API usage is billed separately.

Scores are remembered on this computer for up to 30 days (up to 2,000 posts) so the same post is not billed twice, even after Chrome restarts. Only a hash and the score are kept, never the post text. Changing the provider or your definition of slop starts fresh scores automatically.
