    How this is calculated

    A wrong match means a rule applies to a category you did not choose. A missed match means you chose a category but its score is below or exactly at the threshold.

    Only the latest correction for each identical post counts, using the current provider and definitions. Missing scores are excluded, not negative answers. Analysis uses saved settings; unsaved edits are not used.

    A suggestion needs at least five chosen and five unchosen scored examples. It reduces disagreements without increasing wrong matches on those same examples, but may perform worse on other posts. It changes only your draft until you save.

    What gets saved?

    Only choosing Correct and saving retains the post text and your labels, locally in this profile. Scoring and revealing do not save text here. Corrections are not sent to a provider and do not automatically train the model or change your rules.
